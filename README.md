# MedPrep – Medical Entrance Exam Preparation Platform

**MedPrep** is a web-based medical entrance examination preparation platform designed to help students prepare for two major medical entrance exams: **NEET-UG (India)** and **IMAT (International Medical Admissions Test)**.

The platform provides a centralized learning environment where aspirants can access exam-specific syllabi, study materials, practice questions, previous-year questions (where legally permitted), and timed mock tests. It helps students organize their preparation, evaluate their performance, identify weak areas, and track their academic progress through detailed analytics.

MedPrep follows a dual-track architecture, offering separate learning paths, question banks, assessment configurations, scoring systems, and performance analytics for NEET-UG and IMAT while sharing common platform features.

The platform also integrates **Groq-powered AI capabilities** to support AI-assisted question generation, explanations, and personalized study assistance. AI-generated academic content is subject to validation and faculty review before being published for student use.

Built using modern web technologies, MedPrep aims to make medical entrance exam preparation more structured, accessible, interactive, and data-driven for aspiring medical students.

**Key Features:**
- Separate NEET-UG and IMAT preparation tracks.
- Exam-specific syllabus and curriculum management.
- Digital study materials and learning resources.
- Topic-wise and subject-wise question banks.
- Practice tests and timed mock examinations.
- Automated assessment, scoring, and result analysis.
- Performance analytics and weak-topic identification.
- AI-assisted question generation and study support.
- Faculty and institute management capabilities.
- Secure authentication, role-based access, and student progress tracking.

**Technology Stack:** React, TypeScript, Vite, Node.js, Express.js, PostgreSQL, Supabase, and Groq API.
